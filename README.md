# EU cyber compliance deadlines

Open dataset of the compliance dates under NIS2, the Cyber Resilience Act, DORA, the CER Directive, the AI Act and eIDAS 2, plus the NIS2 transposition status of all 27 EU member states. Every entry cites its legal basis and a primary source.

**Last verified: 2026-10-09.** Checked against primary sources (EUR-Lex, the European Commission, national gazettes and authorities) and refreshed monthly.

Interactive version with countdowns: [EU Cyber Compliance Deadline Tracker](https://nis-2-templates.com/deadline-tracker/) (English) · [EU-Cyber-Fristen-Tracker](https://nis-2-richtlinie.com/fristen/) (Deutsch).

## NIS2 transposition by member state

24 of 27 member states have national NIS2 law in force. "CJEU referral" marks states the Commission has taken to the Court of Justice for failing to transpose.

| Country | Status | National law | In force | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Austria | In force | NISG 2026 (BGBl. I Nr. 94/2025) | 2026-10-01 | Adopted by the Nationalrat on 12 Dec 2025; in force since 1 Oct 2026. Initial registration with the Bundesamt für Cybersicherheit is due by 1 Jan 2027 (the authority accepts filings until 4 Jan 2027). |
| Belgium | In force | Loi du 26 avril 2024 | 2024-10-18 | One of the first full transpositions; supervised by the CCB. |
| Bulgaria | In force | Cybersecurity Act, amended (State Gazette 17/2026) | 2026-02-17 | Amendments adopted 5 Feb 2026, in force 17 Feb 2026 with no general transition period; fines are halved for breaches committed before 1 Jun 2026. Since SG 55/2026 the competent ministry is the Ministry of Innovation and Digital Transformation. |
| Croatia | In force | Zakon o kibernetičkoj sigurnosti (NN 14/2024) | 2024-02-15 | Transposed ahead of the EU deadline. |
| Cyprus | In force | Law 60(I)/2025 amending Law 89(I)/2020 | 2025-04-25 | Competent authority: Digital Security Authority (DSA). |
| Czechia | In force | Zákon č. 264/2025 Sb. (ZoKB) | 2025-11-01 | Goes beyond NIS2 on supply-chain rules for strategically significant services. |
| Denmark | In force | NIS 2-loven (Lov nr. 434 af 6. maj 2025) | 2025-07-01 | General act in force since 1 Jul 2025; energy, telecom and certain financial entities are covered by sector-specific laws. |
| Estonia | In force | Küberturvalisuse seadus, amended by RT I, 30.12.2025, 4 | 2026-01-01 | Amending act adopted 10 Dec 2025 and in force since 1 Jan 2026; supervised by the Information System Authority (RIA). Newly covered entities have three years to meet the requirements. |
| Finland | In force | Kyberturvallisuuslaki 124/2025 | 2025-04-08 | In force since 8 Apr 2025. Supervision is split between Traficom and sector authorities; Traficom's National Cyber Security Centre is the single point of contact. |
| France | Pending · CJEU referral | Projet de loi relatif à la résilience des infrastructures critiques et au renforcement de la cybersécurité | – | Senate adopted 12 Mar 2025; Assembly committee text 10 Sep 2025. The plenary debate set for 7 Oct 2026 was withdrawn on 6 Oct with no new date. Referred to the CJEU on 8 Jul 2026. |
| Germany | In force | NIS2UmsuCG (BGBl. 2025 I Nr. 301) / BSIG | 2025-12-06 | Registration with the BSI was due by 6 Mar 2026 (§ 33 BSIG); entities not yet registered must register without delay. |
| Greece | In force | Law 5160/2024 (ΦΕΚ Α' 195/27.11.2024) | – | Published on 27 Nov 2024; essential and important entities register with the National Cybersecurity Authority via gov.gr. |
| Hungary | In force | Act LXIX of 2024 on the cybersecurity of Hungary (Magyar Közlöny 2024/130) | 2025-01-01 | In force since 1 Jan 2025. The SZTFH supervises companies; public bodies are supervised by the national cybersecurity authority (Special Service for National Security). |
| Ireland | Pending · CJEU referral | National Cyber Security Bill | – | Bill not yet introduced in the Oireachtas; listed for priority publication in the Autumn 2026 legislation programme. Referred to the CJEU on 8 Jul 2026. |
| Italy | In force | D.Lgs. 4 settembre 2024, n. 138 (GU n. 230 of 1 Oct 2024) | 2024-10-16 | Supervised by the ACN. Entities listed in 2025 must have the baseline security measures in place by October 2026. |
| Latvia | In force | Nacionālās kiberdrošības likums (Latvijas Vēstnesis 128A, 4 Jul 2024) | 2024-09-01 | In force since 1 Sep 2024; the National Cybersecurity Centre at the Ministry of Defence is the competent authority. Amended with effect from 18 Jun 2026. |
| Lithuania | In force | Kibernetinio saugumo įstatymas Nr. XII-1428, as amended by Law No XIV-2902 (TAR 2024-13535) | 2024-10-18 | Among the four states never sent a formal notice. |
| Luxembourg | In force | Loi du 5 mai 2026 (Mémorial A n° 225) | 2026-05-10 | Self-registration with the ILR was due by 10 Jul 2026. |
| Malta | In force | S.L. 460.41 (LN 71/2025, amended by LN 89/2026) | 2026-01-23 | Order made by LN 71/2025 in April 2025 but only brought into force on 23 Jan 2026 (LN 22/2026); amended by LN 89/2026. The CIP Department is the national supervisory authority. |

| Portugal | In force | Decreto-Lei n.º 125/2025 | 2026-04-03 | In force 3 Apr 2026. Entities must self-identify on the CNCS MyCiber platform; most security measures apply 24 months after the CNCS implementing regulation of 22 Jun 2026. |
| Romania | In force | OUG 155/2024, approved by Law 124/2025 | 2024-12-31 | Emergency ordinance in force since 31 Dec 2024, with DNSC as the competent authority; approved with amendments by Law 124/2025 (in force 10 Jul 2025). |
